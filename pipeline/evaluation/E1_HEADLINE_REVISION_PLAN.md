## Fixed revision E

Keep D's first-current-unrestricted-per-entry rule, personal-name preference, nonpersonal proper-name grammar handling, dictionary-class cue and tie rules. Before grouping first senses:

1. Prefer substantive records over senses tagged `alt-of` or `form-of`; these records describe spelling/form relations rather than a resolved meaning. If all current records on a side are metadata, propose no headline and preserve the original with a reason.
2. Among substantive current records, prefer records without `humorous`, `nonstandard`, `abbreviation` or `ellipsis` tags if alternatives exist. These are explicit source usage qualifiers, not words or meanings fitted individually. Retain all records in the source inventory and secondary output.
3. Within each raw grammatical class, when a substantive source entry exactly matches the stored comparison headword's spelling, omit entries of that class whose spelling differs only by case. Keep unknown entry identities. Do not apply a class's casing preference to another class. Thus a proper-name celestial reading is not rejected merely because an ordinary noun also exists. No headword, row ID, synonym or inflection is rewritten.

These rules address source entry selection, not how frequent each sense is. They cannot choose between two ordinary senses within the same entry, such as an abdominal organ versus region, wet material versus liquid substance, or scalp hair versus mammal hair generally.

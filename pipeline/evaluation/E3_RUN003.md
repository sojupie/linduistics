## Result and decision

The final variant recovers **five reviewed source-supported relationships across three language pairs** that normalized root equality misses. The frozen recovery gate passes. No confirmed whole-word historical claim was introduced in the 19 reviewed component controls.

**Decision: accept this source-scoped evidence layer for reserved-family evaluation.** It does not replace learner labels, alter meanings, or authorize publication. Broad shared-source statements remain unspecified transmission; source uncertainty and name/common-word entry differences remain visible. An unknown path is not evidence of unrelatedness.

This is a small source-evidence recovery result, not a population precision claim. The open references are source-guided agent judgments, and most historical sources overlap with the dictionary being evaluated.

## Controls, coverage, and failures

The root control supports 55 of the 207 open cases. The variant records some historical relationship in 60, including qualified component and possible relationships. It supports 55 cases with at least one confirmed non-component record. These totals do not measure accuracy and should not be interpreted as vocabulary proportions.

The source index contains 710,110 entry identities. Of 1,609 requested development endpoint identities, 1,451 resolve exactly; 17 additional uniquely resolved ancestors are loaded. Missing entries are retained as unresolved, not replaced with the first same-spelling entry. Source identity candidates remain ambiguous.

The open-case evidence contains 99 source-entry-unavailable records, 1,039 unavailable-ancestor records, 58 ambiguous cognate-target records, 18 ambiguous transmission-target records, and four missing-target records. These are repeated path events, not counts of distinct failed words. The 158 unresolved endpoint identities span the complete development input, not just the open panel.

Five cases receive component-source observations, comprising 11 evidence records. The named controls entomolin/entomolog, filletante/tant, and hemoglobin/hemoglobin remain component-scoped. Such records do not count as inherited whole-word relationships.

Two open cases carry recorded unknown source status: Lesotho and kue/kuva. Their original prose and status are attached to each relationship. The Swedish kuva source explicitly proposes alternatives; it is not converted into a confirmed shared origin. Record-level uncertainty can concern only a transmission route, as with Lesotho, so consumers must retain that qualification rather than equating every caveat with unrelatedness.

The final path code rejects punctuation-only placeholders, stops at unresolved or ambiguous ancestors, and detects cycles. Checks cover inheritance, borrowing, unequal depths, absent and ambiguous entries, source uncertainty, direct cognate statements, shared components, and German capitalization.

## Expert concept panel

Among 995 found development concept cases, source relationships occur in 542 of 662 pairs with the same expert cognate set and in 30 of 333 with different sets. The remaining 120 same-set cases lack a graph relationship. These are observations under a broader source-relationship definition, not a binary cognate accuracy score.

All 30 different-set disagreements were inspected by relation type and source identity. They include shared borrowing sources, different levels of derivation, and name/common-word contamination. Himmel/sky includes an English Sky name entry borrowed from German Himmel; that source record does not establish the ordinary sky noun as a loan from German. Flower/blomst shares a broad recorded PIE source rather than an asserted uninterrupted inheritance path. Bark cases include vessel and tree-cover homographs. These examples explain why a nonempty relationship list cannot automatically become a whole-word cognate label.

The frozen concept panel also retains its previously reported capitalization and ss/ß identity limitations. Final product-label assessment must use the relevant entry and meaning context, not simply the presence of any historical record.

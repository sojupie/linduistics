# H1 case account

Every gain and loss under the recorded binary headline judgment is listed below. Full source-guided observations include unchanged, qualified and unresolved cases in the run directory. All judgments were recorded before decoding version identities.

## Reserved

### da give → de geben · gain

Original: to give ↔ to present; to put.

F: to give ↔ to give (changing ownership).

A specifies transfer of ownership; B's present/put wording is broader and lacks that constraint.

### da hård → de hart · gain

Original: severe, harsh ↔ severe, harsh.

F: hard ↔ hard.

Severe/harsh is not material hardness; B explicitly supplies hard.

### da flue → en fly · gain

Original: fly (insect) (Brachycera) ↔ A lightweight fishing lure resembling an insect..

F: fly (insect) (Brachycera) ↔ Any insect of the order Diptera; characterized by having two wings (except for some wingless species), also called true flies..

A supplies the insect; B supplies a fishing lure.

### da aske → sv aska · gain

Original: to ash (to knock the ash off a lit cigarette, cigar, etc.) ↔ to ash, to remove ashes from a cigarette, smoking pipe, stove, or burner.

F: ash (solid remains of a fire) ↔ ashes, ash.

A selects combustion residue; B selects the ash-removal verb instead of the noun concept.

### da leve → sv leva · gain

Original: to live off or live on ↔ to live on, to live off (something).

F: to live, be alive ↔ to live, to be alive (concretely or figuratively).

A describes being alive; B selects the narrower live-off/sustenance construction.

### da sten → sv sten · gain

Original: a male given name from Old Norse ↔ a male given name from Old Norse.

F: stone ↔ a stone; a rock.

A selects stone/rock; B selects male given names.

### de tun → en do · loss

### de riechen → en smell · gain

### de machen → en do · loss

Original: to do, perform, carry out (an action) (to execute; to put into operation) ↔ To perform; to execute..

F: to make, to produce, to create (an object, arrangement, situation, etc.) ↔ A syntactic marker in a question whose main verb is not another auxiliary verb or be..

A supplies performing an action; B's English auxiliary does not express the concept.

### de alt → en old · gain

## Broad

### da robot → de roboten · gain

Original: robot ↔ to toil.

F: robot ↔ to perform feudal labour.

The German toil reading is dated/colloquial; feudal labor is a substantive historical topic reading. Neither means robot.

### da tosse → en loony · gain

Original: fool, fool about, fool around ↔ An insane or very foolish person..

F: simpleton ↔ An insane or very foolish person..

Simpleton/person aligns; a fool-around verb paired with a person noun changes grammatical use.

### da mandola → en manhole · gain

Original: mandola ↔ The anus of man, in a sexual context..

F: mandola ↔ A hole in the ground used to access the sewers or other underground vaults and installations..

Sexual slang for anus is secondary; sewer-access opening is an ordinary manhole reading. Neither means mandola.

### da forfine → nb forsinke · gain

Original: to refine, fine-tune ↔ to galvanise (UK) or galvanize (coat metal with zinc (sink)).

F: to refine, fine-tune ↔ to delay.

Delay is an ordinary reading; galvanize is a specialist homograph. Neither means refine.

### da spare → sv sparare · loss

Original: to save up ↔ saver (person who saves money).

F: spare (the act of knocking down all remaining pins in second ball of a frame) ↔ saver (person who saves money).

Save and saver are derivationally related; the bowling spare noun is a secondary introduction and loses that relation.

### da besvare → sv bespara · gain

Original: to answer, reply to ↔ to put aside (money or the like).

F: to answer, reply to ↔ to spare, to save (make not have to deal with).

Spare someone something is ordinary; the put-money-aside reading is tagged rare. Neither directly means answer.

### de Provenzalisch → en occitan · loss

Original: Provençal (dialect of the Occitan language) ↔ An endangered Romance language spoken in Occitania, a region of Europe that includes Southern France, Auvergne, Limousin, and some parts of Catalonia and Italy..

F: Provençal, Occitan ↔ Someone of the regional culture that speaks this language; an inhabitant of Occitania..

Occitan person mismatches the German language use. Provençal dialect versus Occitan language is scoped overlap. Preferred word introductions remain ambiguous.

### de RAM → en ram · gain

Original: RAM (random-access memory) ↔ A battering ram; a heavy object used for breaking through doors..

F: RAM (random-access memory) ↔ A male sheep, typically uncastrated..

Battering ram is a valid secondary reading; male sheep is an ordinary ram reading. Neither is the recorded German memory acronym.

### de Tableau → en table · gain

Original: table, chart, ranking ↔ To tabulate; to put into a table or grid..

F: tableau (arrangement of actors on stage or screen) ↔ An item of furniture with a flat top surface raised above the ground, usually on one or more legs..

The Austrian table/chart reading is regional and paired with a verb; the stage arrangement and furniture readings are substantive but different. Full inventories have chart-related overlap.

### de Information → nb opplysning · gain

Original: information (the act of informing or the state of being informed) ↔ information.

F: a piece of information; (in the plural) information, intelligence (things to be known about a topic) ↔ information.

Act/state of informing is a narrower abstract use; information content aligns directly with the Norwegian information reading.

### de Wasser → nb tiss · gain

Original: urine ↔ pee.

F: water (H₂O) ↔ pee.

Urine/pee is a valid secondary match. Water/pee gives ordinary introductions but deliberately loses that selected overlap; retain the secondary evidence.

### en battir → nb batteri · gain

Original: A village in Bethlehem governorate, West Bank, Palestine. ↔ battery (a coordinated group of artillery).

F: A village in Bethlehem governorate, West Bank, Palestine. ↔ battery.

The general battery reading is substantive; artillery battery is a specialized sense. Neither means the Palestinian village.

### en milson → sv milsten · gain

Original: A suburb of Palmerston North, Manawatū-Whanganui, New Zealand. ↔ a milestone.

F: A small village and civil parish (served by Milson and Neen Sollars Parish Council) in southern Shropshire, England (OS grid ref SO6373). ↔ a milestone made of stone.

A stone milestone is an ordinary concrete reading; the figurative Swedish reading is tagged rare. The English places do not share its meaning.

### nb fly → sv fly · gain

Original: to fly ↔ to pass, to go by (of time).

F: to fly ↔ to flee, to run away, to escape.

Time passing is figurative; flee is an ordinary Swedish reading. Neither selected pair directly equates Norwegian flying with Swedish fleeing/time passing.

### nb grei → sv grej · gain

Original: nice, kind, good, good-natured ↔ thing (subjective liking).

F: straight, easy ↔ a thing (concrete or abstract – also of events, like in English).

Straight/easy and thing are substantive uses; the subjective 'one's thing' reading is secondary. Nice/kind is also an ordinary Norwegian reading, but is not equivalent.

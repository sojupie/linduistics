# E2-002: dictionary evidence recovers overlap, but endpoint senses still need checks

Run on `e2`, 2026-10-03. Implementation committed before scoring at `39cb791`.

## Hypothesis and controlled change

Explicit translated glosses and validated English source-sense references can recover meaning overlap missed by dense scores. The variant retains the same encoder matrices, thresholds, source entries, display selection, historical fields, candidate comparisons, and frozen reference mapping. Newly recovered word-level overlap is partial, not exhaustive equivalence.

## Results

| Method | Compatible labels / 197 scorable cases | Percentage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legacy control | 80 | 40.6% |
| Dense-only E2-001 | 118 | 59.9% |
| Always partial overlap | 114 | 57.9% |
| Dictionary-evidence E2-002 | 169 | 85.8% |

The gain over legacy labels is 45.2 percentage points (paired family 95% interval 36.8–53.3). The gain over dense-only is 25.9 points (18.5–33.3). The gain over always-partial is 27.9 points (20.5–35.2). These are compatibility measures against source-guided agent judgments, not independently measured human accuracy.

The variant gained 57 previously incompatible dense-only cases and lost six. Against legacy labels it gained 97 and lost eight. All 28 remaining incompatible predictions and ten unresolved reference cases were inspected against their saved source reasons. The four generic-name controls and two whole-compound controls passed. No subgroup with at least 20 scorable cases crossed the saved five-point loss flag.

Actionable label coverage is 194/207, versus 175/207 for legacy and 193/207 for dense-only. Seven name-identity warnings and six missing-modern-sense warnings are excluded. Earlier E2-001 coverage counts included warnings; they do not establish resolved meanings.

The separate 995-case concept panel yields 849 partial-overlap labels, 128 equivalent labels, 12 related-form labels, five different-meaning labels, and one unverified-name warning. This checks positive coverage only; it does not measure whole-word equivalence or validate partial labels on negative examples. The frozen concept panel retains 14 German capitalization mismatches and four ss/ß differences.

## What worked and what failed

Translated glosses recover ordinary matches that the encoder undervalues: sowing, winter, kneading, alder, aardvark, and weekdays. Recovery is not a word-specific patch; it uses the same grammatical-class and lexical-evidence rules for all cases.

Source-reference validation resolves 623 of 624 requested English identities. The unresolved whore noun identity remains excluded. Validation checks the source record exists and is usable. It does not establish that each endpoint entry uses that source meaning.

The six new errors compared with dense-only are band/bindel, tante/tant, verletzen/invadera, by/by, Obersattel/mutter, and vindue/vindöga. A musical band and a strap share an English translation word, but not its sense. Instrument and fastener nuts expose the same problem. Translation links can also refer to a retired meaning while another modern sense of that grammatical class exists. Checking only modern POS availability therefore overstates modern overlap.

The small secondary/historical group worsened from six compatible legacy labels to three of seven. Although below the automatic subgroup-size threshold, every loss is retained and reviewed. It is a reason to revise the method. Dense-score errors also remain: fact/reality and Israelite/Israeli are still overmatched; hemoglobin and fringe correspondences are missed. Related grammatical forms are not consistently distinguished from disjoint meanings.

## Decision

**Revise; do not adopt this implementation.** The numerical development gates pass, and the gain over the trivial control supports using dictionary evidence as an additional signal. The current endpoint validation still creates systematic polysemy and inactive-sense errors. Passing aggregate gates does not resolve those failures.

A follow-up meaning experiment should validate source evidence against the actual endpoint sense or entry, retain contradictory qualifications, and distinguish current from inactive translation uses. Save that plan before scoring. Do not lower cosine thresholds or add exceptions for these words. Keep the original E2-001 and E2-002 results. E3 can now test historical evidence independently, using unchanged baseline meaning/display inputs because no E2 variant is adopted.

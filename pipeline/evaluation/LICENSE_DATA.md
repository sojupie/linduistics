# Evaluation data: licenses and attribution

These notices cover source material reproduced in the evaluation packets and case files. They do not change the license of the Python code.

- **Dictionary definitions and etymology:** Wiktionary contributors, extracted through Kaikki/Wiktextract. Saved entry identifiers, language codes, and headwords identify the source entries. Dictionary content and evaluation files adapting it retain [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The supplemental Swedish `hatt` check cites its [specific Wiktionary revision](https://sv.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=hatt&oldid=4107160).
- **Concept definitions and expert cognate annotations:** IE-CoR 1.1, Heggarty, Paul; Anderson, Cormac; Scarborough, Matthew (2024), Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology, [IE-CoR](https://iecor.clld.org). Original content is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Case selection, pipeline matches, and agent judgments are additions by this project, not annotations endorsed by IE-CoR.
- **Frequency tokens and counts:** [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), revision `525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6`, sourced from OpenSubtitles2018. Content retains CC BY-SA 4.0; FrequencyWords code is MIT licensed. These are sampled subtitle tokens, not independently established lemma frequencies.

Project-created judgments identify their reviewer and supporting sources. They are distinct from the published source annotations. Additional dictionary checks linked in individual judgments retain their source attribution; short explanatory notes are project-written paraphrases.

# Games

Open **Games** to choose a game. Each game has its own page.

## Guess the Real Meaning

Read a target word and choose its real definition from four options. One option may use a misleading meaning from the source language. Choose a language pair and filter by recorded origin or part of speech. Blind Deck, Target Only, and Assisted change which clues are shown.

## Cognate Grid

Choose the true cognates in a grid of word pairs. The next grid asks for false friends. The pair data comes from the comparative lexicon.

## Sort the Pairs

Classify each pair as a true cognate, related words with changed meanings, or similar-looking words with no shared origin. The game uses the recorded category and etymological relation.

## Find the Odd Word Out

Three words share a recorded root. Choose the word from a different root. The choices come from cognate clusters.

## Build a Word Family

Start with a word and choose the other words from its recorded root family. The words come from cognate clusters.
